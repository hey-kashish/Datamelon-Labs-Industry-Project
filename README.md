### 2. Regional Sales & Category Performance Breakdown
Deep dives into regional distributions, top selling categories via funnel analysis and a comparative performance matrix across geographical territories.

# • Executive Summary of Loyalty Performance Analysis | Power BI

An enterprise-grade end to end retail intelligence dashboard providing a rolling 12-month operational overview. This multi-page analytical tool evaluates core commercial metrics, tracking time series performance, customer loyalty penetration, geographic footprints, operational formats and Pareto customer segments.

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture

### 1. Executive Summary & Trend Analysis
Monitors primary commercial high-level KPIs and tracks long-term variance. It evaluates gross sales metrics against a segmented performance layer tracking specialized loyalty program trends over a 12 months calendar horizon.

### 2. Regional & Operational Infrastructure Performance
Maps sales contributions across multiple geographical territories and top urban markets while auditing the performance of various store operational formats and tier allocations.

### 3. Departmental Share & Pareto Category Breakdown
Deploys product hierarchy matrices to track key category performance clusters, identifying the specific product lines driving the vast majority of gross operational revenue.

### 4. Behavioral Customer Segmentation Analysis
Analyzes user engagement by cross referencing customer program tiers against transactional choices, including preferred checkout modes and primary shopper age demographics.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & Features Tracked

### 💼 High-Level Business KPIs (Rolling 12 Months)
* **Total Gross Revenue:** ₹895.3M (Showing a strong **▲36.8%** 3-month growth window).
* **Total System Volume:** 1.1M Transactions alongside an active pool of **14,345 Shopped Customers**.
* **Value Indicators:** Average Transaction Value of **₹1,740** and a Spend Per Customer metric tracking at **₹34,670**.

### 💳 Loyalty Program Performance
* **Loyalty Contribution:** Accounts for **₹497.3M** in sales and **443.8K** core transactions.
* **Loyalty Distribution:** Program dominated by Silver Tier members (**74.8%**), followed by Gold (**18.4%**) and Platinum (**6.9%**).

### 🗺️ Infrastructure & Retail Footprint
* **Top Sales Geographies:** Driven strongly by the **North Region (₹238M / 26.58%)**, with high performance in city hubs like Bangalore (₹71.7M) and Kolkata (₹71.4M).
* **Format Concentration:** **Supermarkets** lead operations, accounting for **71.0%** of total footprint distribution and **₹639.4M** in total revenue.

### 🏷️ Product & Category Insights (Pareto Distribution)
* **Top Driving Departments:** Led by **Grocery & Staples** (₹296.1M / 33.3% Sales Contribution) and **Beverages** (₹179.5M / 20.0% Sales Contribution).
* **Core Concentration:** Identifies the top **16 highly concentrated categories** (led by Rice & Grains and Tea & Coffee) driving a combined **80% of total business revenue**.

### 👥 Customer Demographics & Preferences
* **Payment Ecosystem:** Dominated by digital methods, led by **UPI at 46.4%**, followed by Cash (**37.4%**) and Cards (**16.2%**).
* **Age Vector:** Core shopping segment sits within the prime **26–35 age group (29.2%)** and the **36–45 group (27.8%)**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop / Service
* **Data Engineering & Modeling:** Power Query, Star Schema Architecture, Advanced DAX (Time-Intelligence, Rolling Periods, Cumulative Pareto Totals)
* **Data Hosting:** SQL Server / Cloud Data Warehouse




# • Overview of Geographical Regional Analysis | Power BI

An advanced geospatial and performance monitoring dashboard to analyze  growth trends across different states, cities and tiers. This project helps businesses evaluate Year over Year (YOY) performance, track city concentration and analyze brand equity against average selling prices.

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture

### 1. Regional Overview & Brand Balancing
Highlights high-level performance indicators (Sales, Volume, Basket Value), maps top revenue-generating states against their average transaction values, and evaluates brand market share.

### 2. State Growth Matrix & Urban Distribution
Features a deep dive performance table capturing YOY growth trends, city level distributions and sales shares categorized across distinct market tiers.

## 📈 Key Metrics & Features Tracked

### 🗺️ High-Level Regional KPIs
* **Total Regional Sales:** 363.78M
* **Total Transactions:** 436K
* **Average Transaction Value (ATV):** 833.83
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 2M
* **Customers Shopped:** 14K
* **Spend Per Customer (SPC):** 26.09K

### 🏢 State Performance & YOY Growth Trends
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** Uttar Pradesh (38M sales, 822 ATV), West Bengal (29M sales, 1035 ATV) and Rajasthan (25M sales, 980 ATV).
* **Overall Metrics (Total Matrix):** Evaluates total mapped state revenue (~261.4M) showing a robust **24.05% YOY Sales Growth** and **21.37% YOY Transaction Growth** overall.

### 🏢 Brand & Market Concentration Analysis
* **Brand Volume Balancing:** Evaluates top brands like Amul, FreshMart, Tata, Nescafe, Coca-Cola, Daawat and India Gate by plotting Sales against Average Selling Price (with bubble sizes indicating units sold).
* **Top Performing Cities:** Highlights high-volume cities led by Noida (85M), Jaipur (76M) and Guwahati (68M).
* **Sales Share Across Tiers:** Visualizes market breakdown by tiers with Tier 2 holding the majority share at **589.45M (52.17%)**.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI
* **Data Sources:** Regional  Database
* **Data Transformations:** Power Query / SQL / DAX Growth Formulations
